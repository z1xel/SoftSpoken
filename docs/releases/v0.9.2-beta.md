## SoftSpoken v0.9.2-beta

A focused update to **Places**, expanding and refining how players interact with the world through its map, making geographical exploration and lore reading more intuitive, consistent, and responsive.

### Highlights

- **More interactive locations on the World Map:** Added 141 interactive map positions across 36 maps, providing additional ways to hover over and read the stories of existing locations, including places without native map highlights. These additions preserve the original appearance of the World Map without introducing additional icons or artificial highlights.
- **Refined World Map tooltips:** More compact tooltips display the location's name, its surrounding region, and an invitation to read its story, without unnecessarily obstructing the map.
- **More accurate location highlighting:** Improved the handling of shared exploration artwork, preventing unrelated locations from being highlighted when they share map textures.
- **Consistent map interactions:** Hovering, clicking, and highlighting now follow the same location-resolution rules, respecting discovery restrictions and prioritizing the game's native map interactions.
- **Improved responsiveness:** Streamlined the World Map's location-detection system, eliminating redundant lookups and unnecessary processing.
- **Refined lore panel placement:** The World Map lore button now remains outside the map itself, avoiding overlap with its contents.
- **Writings cleanup:** Removed the redundant voice-pack download-address button from the settings.

### Installation

Download **SoftSpoken-v0.9.2-beta.zip** below and extract the four addon folders directly into `Interface\AddOns`: `SoftSpoken`, `SoftSpoken_Stories`, `SoftSpoken_Places`, and `SoftSpoken_Writings`.

When upgrading, remove or replace the previous SoftSpoken addon folders. Keep your WTF/SavedVariables and separately installed compatible voice packs. Your existing discoveries, settings and quest history are retained. Do not mix modules from different SoftSpoken versions.

**ClassicAPI is required.** Use its [latest official release](https://github.com/brues-code/ClassicAPI/releases/latest) and enable **Load out of date AddOns** at character selection.

**Voice packs are installed separately**; recordings are not included in this ZIP.

The new static map positions are based on OctoWoW's map data. Compatibility with other clients' custom map layouts may vary; cross-client live validation is not yet complete.

Consult the [README](https://github.com/z1xel/SoftSpoken/blob/main/README.md) for detailed installation instructions and voice-pack downloads.
