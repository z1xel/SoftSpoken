Existing reviewed overlay owners are excluded even before exploration; known native coverage must never be overridden. Static DBCs alone do not establish the exact result of UpdateMapHighlight or ClassicAPI precise hit-testing on undiscovered maps. Therefore every remaining position is classified staged_requires_native_probe, not a proven missing native hit. Optional --native-hit-evidence accepts JSON with input_sha256 matching the generator output and covered records of {map_id,poi_id,canonical,undiscovered:true}; confirmed hits are excluded. No such live positive observations supplied in this pass. G6.3/G6.4 must preserve native priority and decline ambiguous hits. No TOC/XML or map resolver changes; static registry remains unloaded.
